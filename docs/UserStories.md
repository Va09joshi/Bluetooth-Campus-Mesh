# User Stories

---

## Student

* As a student, I want to log in securely so that I can access campus services.
* As a student, I want to discover nearby users so that I can connect with classmates.
* As a student, I want to send messages offline so that communication continues without internet.
* As a student, I want to find departments and offices quickly.
* As a student, I want to mark my attendance easily.

---

## Faculty

* As a faculty member, I want to manage attendance efficiently.
* As a faculty member, I want to broadcast announcements to students.
* As a faculty member, I want to communicate with nearby students.

---

## Visitor

* As a visitor, I want to locate departments and offices.
* As a visitor, I want to navigate the campus easily.

---

## Administrator

* As an administrator, I want to manage users and campus information.
* As an administrator, I want to send emergency alerts.
* As an administrator, I want to monitor platform usage and reports.

---

## Summary

| Role          | Primary Goal                                     |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Student       | Connect, communicate, and access campus services |
| Faculty       | Manage classes and communicate efficiently       |
| Visitor       | Discover campus locations easily                 |
| Administrator | Manage and monitor the campus platform           |

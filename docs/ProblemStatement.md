# Problem Statement

> **Campus communication should not stop when the internet does.**

---

## Background

Most campus services—including communication, navigation, attendance, and announcements—depend heavily on Wi-Fi or mobile internet. During network outages, congestion, or poor coverage, these essential services become unreliable, affecting students, faculty, and visitors.

---

## Problem

Current campus applications:

* Depend on continuous internet connectivity.
* Lack reliable offline communication.
* Make it difficult to discover departments and offices without network access.
* Do not provide resilient emergency communication during outages.
* Offer fragmented user experiences across different campus services.

---

## Proposed Solution

**CampusMesh** is an **offline-first smart campus platform** that uses **Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) mesh networking** to keep users connected even without internet access.

The platform enables:

* Offline messaging
* Nearby user discovery
* Department and office discovery
* Campus directory
* Attendance synchronization
* Emergency alerts
* Automatic data synchronization when connectivity returns

---

## Expected Impact

| Challenge                  | CampusMesh Solution               |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Internet dependency        | Offline BLE mesh communication    |
| Difficult campus discovery | Smart campus directory and search |
| Communication failures     | Peer-to-peer messaging            |
| Delayed emergency alerts   | Local BLE emergency broadcasts    |
| Fragmented campus services | Unified offline-first platform    |

---

> **CampusMesh ensures that essential campus services remain available—online or offline.**

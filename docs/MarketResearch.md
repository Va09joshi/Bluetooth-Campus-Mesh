## Executive Summary

Most campus applications rely on continuous internet connectivity for communication, navigation, attendance, and notifications. During network outages or poor connectivity, these services become unreliable. CampusMesh addresses this gap with an **offline-first platform powered by Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) Mesh networking**, enabling essential campus services to function even without internet access.

---

# Problem in the Current Market

| Challenge                    | Impact                                              |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Internet dependency          | Services stop during outages or weak connectivity.  |
| Fragmented campus systems    | Users switch between multiple applications.         |
| Limited offline support      | Communication and attendance fail without internet. |
| Poor emergency communication | Delayed alerts during network disruptions.          |
| Difficult campus navigation  | Visitors struggle to find departments and offices.  |

Educational institutions are increasingly adopting digital campus solutions, but most existing platforms are cloud-first and require stable internet access. An offline-first platform provides a unique value proposition for campuses with unreliable connectivity or large physical areas.

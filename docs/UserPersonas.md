# User Personas

---

## Student

**Goal**

* Communicate, attend classes, and discover campus services.

**Needs**

* Offline messaging
* Attendance
* Department search
* Campus navigation

---

## Faculty

**Goal**

* Manage classes and communicate with students.

**Needs**

* Attendance management
* Announcements
* Student communication

---

## Visitor

**Goal**

* Navigate the campus easily.

**Needs**

* Office discovery
* Department information
* Indoor navigation

---

## Administrator

**Goal**

* Manage and monitor the campus platform.

**Needs**

* User management
* Analytics
* Emergency broadcasts
* System configuration

---

## Summary

| Persona       | Primary Goal                              |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Student       | Stay connected and access campus services |
| Faculty       | Manage academic activities efficiently    |
| Visitor       | Find locations and information quickly    |
| Administrator | Operate and monitor the campus platform   |
